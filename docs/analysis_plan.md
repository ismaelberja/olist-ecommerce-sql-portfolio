# Business analysis plan

## Stakeholder questions

A commercial manager wants to identify the strongest sales drivers. A customer-experience manager wants to understand delivery reliability and reviews. A CRM analyst wants to estimate how many buyers return.

## Checks before recommendations

1. Confirm the observation window from the original orders table and identify partial months.
2. Compare metrics using consistent grains (order / item / buyer / payment).
3. Report the denominator for percentages, especially delivery punctuality and repeat purchase rate.
4. Confirm whether trends are driven by seasonal events or partially observed months.
5. Treat late delivery and low review scores as **associated**, not necessarily causal.
6. Compare large and small regions with adequate sample sizes; do not rank tiny groups without caveats.

## Findings template — complete only after running the original dataset

- **Sales trends:** [actual months and values]. Are the earliest or latest months partial?
- **Product mix:** [actual top categories and share]. Are there outliers?
- **Repeat customers:** [actual counts and share]. How does the observation window limit the interpretation?
- **Delivery:** [actual rates and measured denominators]. Which differences merit investigation?
- **Reviews:** [actual ratings by delay group]. What confounding variables remain?

## Possible action areas

After calculating real results, assess opportunities in logistics monitoring, retention campaigns, category prioritization and seller performance. No recommendation should be asserted as a finding without numeric support.
